## The Core Challenge: The Chicken-and-Egg Problem

TAGS is a two-sided marketplace. PMs without contractors = unusable product. Contractors without PMs = wasted time. The sequencing of acquisition is the single most important strategic decision in this plan.

**The Rule:** Always have contractor density before PM density. A PM who posts a project and gets no bids will churn forever. A contractor who signs up and sees no projects will stop checking in. The worst outcome is simultaneous zero-zero. The second worst is PM-heavy with no contractor coverage.

**The Strategy:** Launch TAGS in concentric circles. Start contractor acquisition 4–6 weeks before PM acquisition in each geographic zone. Prove contractor density first, then open the PM floodgates.

## Phase 1: Contractor Seeding (Weeks 2–6)

**Goal:** 80–120 verified contractors on the platform before first PM goes live.

### Why contractors first:
- Contractors have nothing to lose by signing up — they're already paying for leads on Angi/HomeAdvisor at high margins
- Their sign-up requires less trust-building than a PM (PMs have to change their workflow; contractors just want jobs)
- Contractor density unlocks PM acquisition — you can truthfully say "X contractors in your area ready to bid"

### Channels:

**1. Mo's Direct Outreach (Highest ROI)**
- Mo calls contractors personally. "Hey, I'm building a platform specifically for apartment contractors in KC. No subscription fee — you only pay 1.5% when you get paid. I want you in early before it's crowded."
- Target: 5–10 calls/day for 3 weeks. Mo has the personality for this.
- Dale's network — any contractor connections he has in KC get called first.
- Goal: 40–60 contractors from personal outreach

**2. Facebook/Nextdoor Contractor Groups**
- KC has active contractor Facebook groups (KC Home Improvement Pros, KC Contractors Network, etc.)
- Post a plain-language intro: "Building a marketplace for apartment work in KC. No lead fees, no subscriptions. Contractors get in free. Interested?" → DM to sign up.
- Boosted post: $300–500 targeting self-employed contractors in KC metro

**3. Angi / HomeAdvisor / Thumbtack Harvesting**
- Search these platforms for every KC contractor in target trades
- Build outreach list → send personal email/DM: "You're paying 15–40% for leads. We charge 1.5% only when you're paid. Want to be first on the platform?"

**4. Google Ads — Contractor**
- Keywords: "apartment repair jobs Kansas City", "multifamily contractor leads KC", "property management contractor"
- Budget: $500/month
- Landing page: contractor-specific, emphasizes free sign-up and quality leads

**5. Trade Association Presence**
- Contact the Kansas City Homebuilders Association and NARI (National Association of the Remodeling Industry) KC chapter
- Ask to attend a meeting or send a rep (Mo or Dale) to introduce TAGS
- Sponsor a lunch or a newsletter blurb ($200–500) — gets you in front of 50–100 active contractors at once

## Phase 2: PM Acquisition Begins (Week 6+)

**Trigger condition:** ≥75 verified contractors live on the platform across at least 5 trades.

**Goal:** 100 paying PMs within 90 days of PM launch.

### Channel Stack (ordered by expected ROI):

---

### 🥇 Channel 1: Mo on Camera — Organic Social

This is the highest-leverage channel. Mo is a natural. Authentic beats polished in this space.

**Content cadence (3–5 posts/week across Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn):**

| Format | Description | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| "PM Problem of the Week" | Mo films a real maintenance issue (with permission), walks through how a SOW should look, shows TAGS generating it in real time. Educational + product demo in one. | 2x/week |
| Contractor spotlight | 60-second video of a TAGS contractor in the field. Shows PMs the quality of people on the platform. | 1x/week |
| Behind-the-scenes | Building TAGS, team decisions, product updates. Humanizes the brand. | 1x/week |
| Industry hot take | Mo shares an opinion on the PM space. "Why your maintenance process is costing you 20% more than it should." Builds authority. | 1–2x/week |

**Platform priority:**
1. **LinkedIn** — PMs, regional managers, and asset managers are all on LinkedIn. Organic reach is still solid for authentic founders. Mo posts as himself, tags TAGS.
2. **Instagram** — Visual platform, good for before/after project content and Mo's energy.
3. **TikTok** — Increasingly how trade/PM professionals find new tools. Short, fast, no fluff.
4. **YouTube** — Long-form. Mo does a "TAGS Platform Walkthrough" and a "Why I Built This" story. These don't need high view counts — they just need to exist so that when a PM Googles TAGS, they can see Mo's face and trust it.
5. **Facebook** — Targeted for older PM demographic and local KC groups.

**AI-generated content to support Mo:**
- Carousels: "5 things every PM should include in a maintenance work order"
- Infographics: "Average cost by trade for apartment repairs in KC"
- Email newsletters with AI-drafted body, Mo reviews and adds one personal line
- Blog posts for SEO: "How to scope apartment repair projects", "Finding reliable contractors in Kansas City"

### 🥈 Channel 2: AAKC (Apartment Association of Kansas City)

This is the single highest-density PM audience in KC. AAKC has hundreds of PM members — multifamily operators, regional managers, and owners.

**Tactics:**
- Become a vendor/affiliate member ($500–2,000/year) — gets you in their directory and newsletter
- **Sponsor a meeting or event** — Mo speaks for 5–10 minutes to the room. Live SOW demo. QR code for sign-up at the door.
- **AAKC newsletter ad** — targeted to exactly the right audience
- **Education session** — propose a lunch-and-learn: "How AI is changing maintenance management in multifamily." AAKC loves free education content. Mo presents, TAGS gets the room.

**Budget estimate:** $2,000–5,000 for the year for full AAKC engagement. This is high-ROI spend.

### 🥉 Channel 3: LinkedIn Targeted Outreach

**Target titles in KC metro:** Property Manager, Community Manager, Regional Property Manager, Asset Manager, Director of Property Management, Maintenance Supervisor, Facilities Manager

### Channel 5: Direct Email Outreach (Cold + Warm)

**Build the list:**
- Scrape AAKC member directory, LinkedIn, Google Maps for KC multifamily PM companies
- Every apartment complex in KC metro has a property manager — these are public records
- Tools: Apollo.io, Hunter.io for email discovery

**Email sequence (5-step):**
1. Day 1: "Hey [name] — I'm Mo, building a KC-specific tool for PMs. Quick intro."
2. Day 4: 60-second Loom from Mo. Personal, not scripted. "Here's what we built and why."
3. Day 8: Social proof — "3 KC PMs tried it last week. Here's what one said."
4. Day 14: "Contractors are ready in your area." Show contractor density stat.
5. Day 21: Last touch. "Final ask — want a free SOW generated for your next project? No strings."

**Volume:** Target 500–1,000 KC PM contacts. At 2–5% conversion = 10–50 sign-ups from cold email alone.

**Tools:** Instantly.ai or Lemlist for sequencing. All sends from a warm domain (not tagyourproject.com main — use outreach.tagyourproject.com or similar to protect deliverability).

---

### Channel 6: Referral Program

**Design:** Every PM who refers another PM who becomes a paying customer gets 1 free month.
Every contractor who refers a contractor gets their next project's fee waived.

**Why this works:** Property managers talk to each other constantly — at AAKC events, in building management circles. One happy PM who talks to three colleagues is worth a thousand LinkedIn impressions.

**Mechanism:** Simple referral link in the dashboard. Atlas tracks conversions.

### Channel 8: Strategic Partnerships

**Property management software integrations:**
- AppFolio, Buildium, Rent Manager, Yardi — PMs already live in these tools
- Even a simple "export your work order to TAGS" integration or a mention in their partner marketplace gets eyes on TAGS at the exact moment a PM has a maintenance problem

**Real estate investor groups:**

- Investors own apartments. If Mo presents at one KC REIA meeting, he's talking to 50+ people who have maintenance problems and nobody to talk to

**Property management consulting firms:**
- Some consultants serve multiple PM companies. One consultant converted = multiple PM accounts
